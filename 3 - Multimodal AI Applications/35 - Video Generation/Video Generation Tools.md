Workflow Tips
Start with a clear brief: target audience, tone, duration, aspect ratio, platform.
Use structured prompts: scene description, subject actions, camera movement, lighting, mood, and any style references.
For avatar content, prepare clean scripts, pronunciation notes, and consistent brand visuals.
For synchronized audio, outline exact beats: narration timing, on-screen events, and sound cues.
Quality Control
Review coherence: subject continuity, limb and object interactions, occlusions, and scene consistency.
Check brand alignment: color, typography overlays, and logo treatment.
Validate accessibility: captions, readable text, and adequate contrast.
Iterate with short drafts before producing longer outputs to control cost and time.
Governance and Compliance
Rights and licensing: stock media, music, likeness permissions, and model usage terms.
Disclosure norms: label synthetic content where required.
Bias and safety: review portrayals across demographics and contexts.
Data privacy: avoid sensitive inputs; follow organizational policies.
Operational Considerations
Cost: per-minute or per-credit pricing can scale quickly; plan for iterations.
Performance: higher resolutions and longer durations increase generation time.
Integration: look for editors, asset libraries, and workflow APIs within the chosen platform.
Localization: leverage multi-language TTS and subtitles for global reach.
