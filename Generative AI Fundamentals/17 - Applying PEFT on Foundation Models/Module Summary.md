Key Takeaways
Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) is a powerful technique for teaching a pre-trained Large Language Model (LLM) a new, specific skill.
Even large, capable LLMs may struggle with niche tasks like precise spelling, making SFT a necessary tool for specialization.
The SFT process involves preparing a specialized dataset of prompt-and-response pairs to guide the model's learning.
By applying SFT, you can create a specialized model that demonstrates improved performance on a target task compared to its original base version.
