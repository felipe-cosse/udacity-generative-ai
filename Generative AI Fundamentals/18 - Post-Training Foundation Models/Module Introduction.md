Foundation models are incredibly powerful, but their initial pre-training can lead to behaviors that aren't ideal for human interaction, such as factual inaccuracies or ethically problematic content. This module dives into the essential post-training processes used to refine these models. We'll explore how to shape their capabilities so their outputs align more closely with human expectations and values, making them safer and more useful for real-world applications.

By the end of this module you will be able to:

Explain why pre-trained foundation models require further refinement for direct human interaction.
Identify common issues in pre-trained models, such as factual inaccuracies and ethically problematic content.
Describe the post-training processes used to align model outputs with human expectations and values.






Foundation models, while incredibly capable after their initial pre training, often exhibit behaviors not ideal for direct human interaction. They might prioritize completing text rather than engaging in a natural conversation, or their outputs could contain factual inaccuracies or ethically problematic content. This lesson will explore the essential processes applied after a model's foundational training to refine its capabilities and ensure its outputs align more closely with human expectations and values.
