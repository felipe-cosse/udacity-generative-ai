The Five-Step Selection Process
Define the Task Type - Is this generation or reasoning?
Profile Performance - Test models on YOUR data, not public benchmarks.
Build TCO Projection - Include all costs: API, infrastructure, development, maintenance.
Assess Risk and Control Requirements - What are your regulatory constraints? Data sensitivity?
Make a Weighted Decision - Which factors matter most for your specific use case?
Adapting to a Rapidly Changing Landscape
The LLM landscape changes incredibly fast. The right model today might be obsolete in six months. The selection process itself is more valuable than any single choice. The best model isn't the one at the top of the leaderboard - it's the one that solves your specific problem within your constraints. Build evaluation capabilities, and you'll always be able to adapt to the evolving landscape of AI models.

Key Takeaways
Start with the problem, not the model - Understand your cognitive requirements first
Match model class to task type - Don't use a generation model for complex reasoning
Evaluate holistically - Speed and cost matter as much as accuracy
Consider total cost of ownership - Include development and maintenance costs
Plan for change - Build evaluation capabilities, not just one-time selections
