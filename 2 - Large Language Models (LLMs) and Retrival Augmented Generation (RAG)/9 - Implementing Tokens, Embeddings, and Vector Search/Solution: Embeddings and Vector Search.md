Key Takeaways
1. Embeddings Capture Meaning
Convert text to numbers that preserve semantic relationships
Similar meanings → similar vectors
Enables mathematical operations on text
2. Cosine Similarity Measures Relevance
Ranges from -1 to 1
0.8+ indicates strong similarity
Works because embeddings are normalized
3. Semantic Search > Keyword Search
Finds relevant content even with different words
"broke" matches "stopped working", "defective", "malfunctioned"
Essential for customer service, search, and recommendations
4. Clustering Reveals Hidden Patterns
Automatically groups similar content
Identifies trends without manual tagging
Scalable to millions of documents
5. Embeddings Are Reusable
Create once, use forever (until content changes)
Save to disk to avoid re-embedding
Costs are minimal ($0.02 per 10K reviews)
6. Real-World Impact
Reduces support ticket resolution time by 40-60%
Identifies product quality issues 2-3 weeks earlier
Improves recommendation accuracy by 30-50%
