Key Takeaways
Prompt complexity should match task complexity: Don't use premium prompts for simple tasks.
Cost-effectiveness beats absolute cost: Quality per dollar matters more than cost alone.
Standard prompts are the sweet spot: For most production applications, standard prompts on standard models optimize the quality-cost-speed triangle.
Volume amplifies everything: Small per-request savings become massive at scale.
Test before deploying: What works in development may not be cost-effective in production.
Monitor continuously: Prompt performance can drift as models update or usage patterns change.
Different tasks need different strategies: Summary, creative writing, and problem-solving all benefit from different prompt approaches.
Marginal improvements have diminishing returns: Going from 0.85 to 0.90 quality may cost 3× more.
