The LLM landscape is vast and rapidly evolving. To navigate it successfully:

Experiment with different models: Each has strengths and weaknesses
Master prompting techniques: Good prompts can 10x your results
Build RAG systems: Ground your applications in factual data
Think in workflows: Combine multiple capabilities for complex tasks
Stay informed: This field advances weekly
