In this course, you'll discover the truth about Large Language Models (LLMs) and learn to build systems that harness their power while avoiding their pitfalls.

You'll develop practical skills to work with LLMs professionally:

Understand how LLMs work: From simple word prediction to complex reasoning
Choose the right model: Balance cost, performance, and capabilities for your needs
Master prompt engineering: Get reliable, high-quality responses from AI
Build Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems: Ground AI responses in factual data to prevent hallucinations
Evaluate system quality: Measure and improve AI performance with industry-standard metrics
What You'll Build
Throughout this course, you'll build real-world applications:

Customer Service AI Assistant: Create an intelligent chatbot that handles customer inquiries with accuracy and context
Semantic Search System: Build a system that finds relevant information using embeddings and vector databases
NASA Mission Intelligence System (Project): Apply everything you've learned to build a RAG-based chat system that answers questions about historic NASA missions
