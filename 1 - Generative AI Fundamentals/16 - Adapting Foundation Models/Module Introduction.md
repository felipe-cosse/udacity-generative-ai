Welcome! Powerful AI models are brilliant generalists, but how do we make them experts on specific, real-world problems? This module tackles that core challenge. You'll learn about "adaptation," the crucial process of customizing a general foundation model to excel at specialized tasks. By understanding adaptation, you'll be able to unlock the full potential of these advanced AI systems to solve unique business problems, from analyzing internal data to recognizing specific objects.

What You'll Learn
Define adaptation as the process of customizing a foundation model for a specific use case.
Explain why generalist AI models require adaptation to solve specialized, real-world problems.
Identify a range of downstream tasks, such as question answering and sentiment analysis, that are enabled by adaptation.





Imagine you have access to one of the most powerful AI systems ever created. It's a brilliant generalist. It can write, summarize and understand vast amounts of information. But what happens when you ask it about your company's internal sales data from last quarter, or to identify a very specific type of defect in your manufacturing process. Out of the box, it will likely struggle. This is a core challenge and the huge opportunity we're going to tackle. How do you turn a powerful generalist model into a specialized expert for your specific needs? This is where adaptation comes in. As you can see here, adapting foundation models refers to the process of customizing these comprehensive AI systems to better suit specific applications or to address up-to-date information? It's how you unlock their full potential and solve real world business problems. Adaptation simply means taking a foundation model and customizing it to fulfill a given use case. What sorts of cases you may ask? Well, to list a few we have answering questions about a topic, determining the sentiment of a piece of text, extracting information from text, an image or even a PDF, writing a caption for an image for increased accessibility, recognizing objects in a provided image, and following instructions in general.
