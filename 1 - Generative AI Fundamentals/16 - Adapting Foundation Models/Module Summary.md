This module explored several powerful strategies for adapting foundation models to specific needs. We covered prompt engineering as a simple first step, retrieval augmented generation (RAG) for incorporating up-to-date information, and fine-tuning for deeper customization. We also discussed model compression to enhance efficiency and performance, and agentic AI, which expands capabilities by integrating external tools and actions. Mastering these methods will equip you to build AI solutions tailored precisely to your requirements.

Key Takeaways
Prompt Engineering: A simple, effective, and often foundational first step for guiding a model's output without altering its underlying structure.
Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG): Enhances foundation models by providing them with external, up-to-date, and domain-specific information, leading to more accurate and relevant responses.
Fine-tuning: A method for deeper customization that involves adjusting the internal weights of a pre-trained model using a smaller, task-specific dataset.
Model Compression: Techniques used to shrink model sizes, which improves efficiency, performance, and accessibility, making them suitable for deployment in resource-constrained environments.
Agentic AI: Expands a model's capabilities by enabling it to use external tools and perform actions, moving beyond simple text generation to accomplish complex tasks.
