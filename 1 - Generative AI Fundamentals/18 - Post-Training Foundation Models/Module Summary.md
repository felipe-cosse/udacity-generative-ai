In this module, we explored why even powerful foundation models require additional refinement after their initial pre-training. We covered key post-training methods, distinguishing between Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) for structured guidance and Preference Fine-Tuning for nuanced alignment. These techniques, including RLHF and DPO, are crucial for transforming general models into powerful, responsible, and effective conversational AI agents that better align with human expectations.

Key Takeaways
Foundation models often need post-training refinement to align with human expectations and values.
Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) provides structured guidance through demonstrations, improving a model's instruction-following and specialized domain knowledge.
Preference Fine-Tuning methods (like RLHF and DPO) allow models to learn from nuanced human judgments and subjective criteria.
Combining these techniques enhances model performance, usability, and alignment, enabling AI systems to interact more naturally, responsibly, and effectively.





Foundation models, despite their impressive initial capabilities from broad pre-training, often require further refinement to align effectively with human expectations and values through methods such as supervised fine tuning or SFT and preference fine tuning, including advanced techniques like reinforcement learning from human feedback, DPO, and GRPO. These models transition from general purpose text completion systems into powerful, interactive and ethically aligned conversational agents. SFT provides structured guidance through explicit demonstrations, improving instruction following and specialized domain knowledge, while preference tuning methods allow models to internalize nuanced human judgments and subjective criteria. Together, these post training steps significantly enhance model performance, usability, and alignment, enabling AI systems to interact more naturally, responsibly, and effectively with the lessons you've learned here.
